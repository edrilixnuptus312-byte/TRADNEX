💡 TRADNEX Strategy & Logic

CORE ENGINE

1. A completed 4H candle must close above the previous 4H high for a BUY setup.

2. A completed 4H candle must close below the previous 4H low for a SELL setup.

3. The completed 4H closing price becomes the key level.

4. On 15M, the candle BODY must close above that level for BUY confirmation.

5. On 15M, the candle BODY must close below that level for SELL confirmation.

6. Signals reset when a new 4H candle begins.
