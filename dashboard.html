TRADNEX METHOD

4H → 15M Strategy

TRADNEX is built around a higher-timeframe 4H structure followed by 15M confirmation. The system does not use 5M confirmation.

01 — 4H Structure

Identify the completed 4H structure and its relevant high, low and body levels.

02 — 15M Confirmation

Wait for a confirmed 15M candle close beyond the saved structure level.

03 — BUY Setup

A BUY confirmation occurs when the confirmed 15M candle closes above the relevant body structure.

04 — SELL Setup

A SELL confirmation occurs when the confirmed 15M candle closes below the relevant body structure.

Signal confidence will be supplied by the TRADNEX signal engine using defined confirmation conditions. It will not be randomly generated.
